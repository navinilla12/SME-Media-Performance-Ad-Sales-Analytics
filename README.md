# SME Media Performance & Ad Sales Analytics

An end-to-end business intelligence project analyzing audience engagement, acquisition channels, advertising campaigns, and modeled ad sales revenue for a digital media business.

The project combines Google Analytics 4 public sample data with modeled advertising-sales data to demonstrate a realistic analytics workflow from data extraction and cleaning through SQL analysis and Power BI reporting.

## Dashboard Preview

### Executive Overview

The Executive Overview summarizes modeled advertising revenue, audience sessions, impressions, engagement rate, and campaign performance.

### Audience Intelligence

The Audience Intelligence page analyzes sessions, users, engaged sessions, engagement rate, acquisition channels, and audience trends.

### Campaign Performance

The Campaign Performance page provides campaign-level analysis across impressions, clicks, CTR, revenue, effective CPM, and contract fulfillment.

### Ad Sales & Revenue Analysis

The Ad Sales & Revenue Analysis page focuses on advertising revenue trends, advertiser contribution, campaign monetization, and revenue versus contract value.
